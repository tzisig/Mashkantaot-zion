## 2. What every competitor has (table stakes)
1. Phone + WhatsApp + short form above the fold
2. Mortgage calculator (often also refinance / eligibility calculator)
3. Separate page per service, including "declined by the bank" and loan consolidation
4. Google reviews / testimonials
5. "Free first consultation" offer

## 3. Where we can be different
1. **Real city pages** - competitors use templates. We write each city page with genuine local content.
2. **Fee transparency** - almost nobody shows prices; forums complain about it. Showing the fee model (even a range) is a trust signal.
3. **Clear process** - no one shows a simple step-by-step timeline.
4. **Answer the doubts openly** - conflict of interest, bank commissions, "do I even need an advisor". Competitors avoid these.
5. **Credentials, stated plainly** - economist since 2007 (verified by owner), no inflated claims.

## 4. What customers doubt and ask (forums + articles)
- **Is it worth the money?** Fees of 5,000-10,000 ILS feel high for a profession with no license.
- **Conflict of interest** - does the advisor get a hidden commission from the bank?
- **Competence** - "took a 2-month course" advisors; generic advice without real analysis.
- **Can't I do it myself?** Banks respond faster today; mortgage can be refinanced later.
- **What satisfied people**: saved time and stress, better rates than expected, help during bank delays.
- **What disappointed people**: generic advice, high fee without clear return.

Typical fees (Midrag price guide): single consultation 500-1,000 ILS; mix building 2,000-3,000; full accompaniment 5,000-10,000; complex case 10,000-15,000+; remote ~half. Usually half upfront, half at signing; not a percentage of the mortgage.

## 5. Regulation (important)
- As of mid-2026 there is **no regulator and no license** for mortgage advisors in Israel.
- A bill to regulate the profession: approved by the Ministerial Committee for Legislation on 2025-11-24; approved by the Knesset Economic Committee for first reading on 2026-05-26.
- Planned: mandatory registration in a registry, certification exam, Ministry of Justice as regulator, code of ethics, conflict-of-interest rules, fines; criminal penalty for impersonating a licensed advisor.
- **Impact on the site:** never say "licensed" / "certified" (מוסמך, מורשה) unless true. Plan a field for a future license number in site config.

## Pricing transparency (checked 2026-09-23)

Eight pages that rank for "כמה עולה יועץ משכנתאות" were opened and read. Only one
publishes the company's own fees. The rest publish market ranges, and two of them
disclaim the ranges explicitly.

| Site | Publishes | Figures |
|---|---|---|
| evensapir.co.il/mortgage-consulting-cost | **Its own fee table** | New mortgage 6,000-9,000; refinance 7,500-9,500; first meeting 500-800. Excl. VAT, standard cases |
| pro.co.il/mortgage-councils/pricing | Directory rates from reported jobs, updated monthly | Mix meeting 2,200-3,000; obtaining a mortgage 5,000-8,000; full accompaniment 5,500-10,000 |
| midrag.co.il/Content/Price/6875 | Directory guide, basis not stated | Single meeting 500-1,000; mix 2,000-3,000; full 5,000-10,000; complex 10,000-15,000+ |
| mym.co.il | Market ranges, disclaimed | States in writing that the figures are not what the firm charges |
| lsmashkanta.co.il | Market ranges, disclaimed | Notes there is no binding fee scale for advisors |
| alpm.co.il, mashkantaguru.co.il, pln.co.il, cantaio.co.il, mashkantaut.co.il | Market ranges only | No own price stated anywhere on the page |

Implication for `/pricing`: publishing an own fee table is close to unoccupied
ground in this niche. The one site that does it pairs each figure with what the
service includes and marks VAT treatment. The same page also claims a savings
figure, which this site will not do (see `LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md`).
