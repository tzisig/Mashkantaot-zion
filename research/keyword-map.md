# Keyword map (v2)

Source: Google Keyword Planner historical metrics, Israel, all languages, Sep 2025 - Aug 2026
(file: `Keyword Stats 2026-09-22 at 18_04_48.csv`, 3,261 keywords).
Volumes are bucketed by Google (no active ad spend): 50,000 = 10K-100K, 5,000 = 1K-10K, 500 = 100-1K, 50 = 10-100.
Distribution: 9 keywords at 10K-100K, 23 at 1K-10K, 141 at 100-1K, 1,019 at 10-100, 2,069 under 10.
Close variants are merged by Google (e.g. "יועץ משכנתאות" is counted under "יועץ משכנתא").
CPC = top-of-page bid range in USD.

## Key findings
1. **Calculator is the biggest traffic opportunity**: "מחשבון משכנתא" 10K-100K, low competition, CPC $0.41-1.41.
2. **Spelling**: "מחזור משכנתא" 1K-10K vs "מיחזור משכנתא" 100-1K. H1 and URL use "מחזור"; title and body cover both.
3. **Highest commercial value**: declined borrowers ("משכנתא למסורבים" up to $43, "הלוואה כנגד נכס למסורבים" up to $53), equity loans ("הלוואה כנגד נכס" up to $41), consolidation ("איחוד הלוואות" 1K-10K, up to $16).
4. **Non-bank loans for declined borrowers is big**: "הלוואה חוץ בנקאית למסורבים" 1K-10K, "הלוואה לכל מטרה למסורבים" 100-1K, "הלוואה חוץ בנקאית לבעלי bdi שלילי" 100-1K.
5. **Reverse mortgage**: "משכנתא הפוכה" 1K-10K, plus many 100-1K and 10-100 variants (what is it, interest, calculator, disadvantages, from what age).
6. **Pricing intent**: "יועץ משכנתא מחיר" 100-1K.
7. **Cities**: only Beer Sheva has 100-1K ("יועץ משכנתא באר שבע"). Ashdod, Ashkelon, Netivot, Rahat, Kiryat Gat: 10-100. Ofakim, Sderot, Omer: under 10. City pages serve GBP and the long tail, not volume.

## Out of scope
- Mortgage insurance (ביטוח משכנתא...) - requires an insurance agent license.
- Lender-brand / instant consumer loans (הלוואה חוץ בנקאית ישראכרט/מקס/כאל/רמי לוי, באישור מיידי, עד 10000) - people looking for a lender, not an advisor.
- Bank navigational terms (אזור אישי, צור קשר, bank + calculator) and competitor brand names.
